Video Games and Interactive Entertainment

Video games are interactive entertainment in which players participate in virtual environments and make decisions that influence their experience. Popular categories include role-playing games, strategy games, puzzle games, simulation games, sports games, and first-person shooters. Modern games often combine graphics, sound, storytelling, challenges, and player choices.

Some games require planning, problem solving, pattern recognition, resource management, and decision making. Strategy and puzzle games are particularly focused on these activities because players must evaluate information and choose actions. Story-driven games can also present ethical choices and multiple outcomes, encouraging players to consider consequences and different perspectives.
