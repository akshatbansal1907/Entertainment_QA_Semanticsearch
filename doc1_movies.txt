Movies and Genres

Movies are a major form of entertainment and can also encourage viewers to think about people, choices, conflicts, and society. Common movie genres include action, adventure, comedy, drama, romance, science fiction, fantasy, thriller, documentary, and historical films. Drama and documentary films can be especially useful when a viewer wants to examine realistic situations, different viewpoints, and the consequences of decisions.

Movies can support thoughtful viewing when the audience actively questions the story instead of only watching for amusement. Films with complex characters, moral dilemmas, social issues, mysteries, historical situations, or multiple viewpoints can encourage analysis and discussion. A viewer can develop deeper thinking by asking why characters act in certain ways, whether the evidence in the story is convincing, and what alternative decisions could have changed the outcome.
