Theater and Live Performance

Theater is a live form of entertainment in which performers present plays, musicals, and other productions directly to an audience. A theatrical production may combine acting, dialogue, music, singing, dancing, costumes, lighting, sets, and stage direction. Famous theater traditions and venues include Broadway and London's West End.

Live performances can encourage audiences to interpret character motivation, conflict, symbolism, and social themes. Because theater happens in real time, viewers can also observe how acting, staging, music, and audience response work together to communicate meaning. Plays that present difficult choices or competing viewpoints can provide material for discussion and critical interpretation.
