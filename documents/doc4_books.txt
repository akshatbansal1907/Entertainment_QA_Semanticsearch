Books and Literary Entertainment

Books are a traditional source of entertainment and include novels, short stories, biographies, poetry, graphic novels, and other forms of literature. Fiction can introduce readers to imaginary worlds and characters, while biographies and other nonfiction works can present real people, events, and ideas. Different literary genres give readers different styles of storytelling and interpretation.

Reading can encourage deeper thinking because readers often have to interpret characters, themes, evidence, symbolism, and the author's point of view. Mystery and detective stories can involve clues and inference, while historical fiction can encourage readers to compare fictional events with their understanding of history. Discussing a book's argument, characters, and evidence can make reading a more analytical activity.
