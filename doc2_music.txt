Music and Musical Genres

Music is a widely enjoyed form of entertainment that combines rhythm, melody, harmony, instruments, and vocals. Major genres include pop, rock, classical, jazz, hip-hop, folk, electronic music, and country music. Different genres use different musical structures and performance styles, giving listeners many ways to experience emotion and artistic expression.

Music can also encourage attentive listening and interpretation. Classical and jazz performances, for example, can invite listeners to notice patterns, changes, improvisation, and relationships between musical elements. Lyrics in songs may also communicate stories, emotions, or social ideas. Discussing why a song creates a particular effect can help a listener practise observation and interpretation.
